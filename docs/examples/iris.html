Neural Flora: Botanical Species Classifier

Adjust the flower sepal and petal measurements in real-time. A 2-layer perceptron (4 inputs → 8 hidden ReLU neurons → 3 Softmax outputs) trained with Neural_Kit.Layer classifies the sample instantly.
